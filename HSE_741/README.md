## Course description

Introduces study design and epidemiologic reasoning for health data analysis. Students examine how disease occurrence is measured, how bias and other explanatory factors affect findings, and how screening or diagnostic performance is assessed. The course uses R.

Summary paraphrased from the [course catalog](https://dartmouth.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/orc/departments-programs-graduate/masters-of-health-science-education/master-of-science-in-health-data-science-online/health-data-science-online/hse-741); checked September 25, 2026.

## Key topics

- Incidence, prevalence, and attributable risk
- Disease latency
- Confounding and effect modification
- Bias, generalizability, and classification metrics

## Place in the program

Helps connect statistical results to the design and limitations of the study that generated them.
