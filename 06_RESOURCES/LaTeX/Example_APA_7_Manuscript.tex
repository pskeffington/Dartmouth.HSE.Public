\section{Method}
\subsection{Design and Data Source}
The worked example is a descriptive file-processing exercise using
\texttt{pseudo\_metadata.csv}. The classroom schema has six fields: an unnamed
first field, sample identifier, condition, age, sex, and batch. The sex field
uses \texttt{F} and \texttt{M}. These labels are source-file encodings and are
not inferred from participant names or other variables.

[Report the data source, acquisition date, inclusion criteria, sample size,
and relevant permissions. For an empirical study, describe the sampling
design and ethics approval or exemption as applicable. For this synthetic
exercise, state that the records are simulated and identify the course source.]

\subsection{Measures and Variables}
[Define the variables relevant to the question, their units, category labels,

\subsection{Procedure}
The procedure inspects the file header, counts lines, retains the header while
selecting \texttt{F}-coded records from field 5, and saves the subset as
\texttt{only\_female.txt}. A copied alias,
\texttt{females\_metadata.csv}, preserves the second filename used in the
exercise. The lab also extracts condition and age into a temporary directory
and removes that directory after inspection. The original input is retained.

[Describe the exact software versions, commands or functions, file locations,
