# Selection rubric for the 60-gene teaching catalog

[Companion](../BREAST_CANCER_60_GENE_COMPANION.md) · [Source register](ANNOTATION_SOURCE_REGISTER.md)

Selection recorded: 10 October 2026, before adding the 39 new entries. This
catalog starts with the 21 symbols in the published descriptor guide and adds
39 distinct approved human genes. It is a teaching selection, not a statistical
signature, clinical panel, PAM50 implementation or collection ranked by an
unpublished experiment.

## Prospective teaching criteria

1. Retain the existing 21 genes so published lessons remain connected.
2. Cover all twelve requested domains, including tissue composition, immune
   cells and a noncoding transcript; do not focus only on tumor-cell drivers.
3. Choose genes that illustrate distinct measurements: DNA sequence, copy
   number, transcript quantity, protein localization or phosphorylation.
4. Require a unique approved HGNC identity and an NCBI Gene cross-reference.
   Add an Ensembl identifier only when the record is unambiguous.
5. Prefer a gene-relevant primary paper, accurately labeling abstract-only
   review. An annotation-supported teaching example may remain useful without
   a verified breast-specific mechanism; state that evidence gap openly.
6. Include contextual or conflicting evidence and distinguish research
   relevance from established clinical applications.
7. Use multiple domain tags when justified. Neither a single tag nor a single
   transcript establishes a molecular subtype.

## Public evidence informing the balance

- [TCGA molecular portraits (2012)](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature11412)
  motivates learning across genomic, RNA and protein measurements rather than
  treating them as interchangeable.
- [Wu et al., single-cell/spatial breast-cancer atlas (2021)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41588-021-00911-1)
  motivates epithelial, fibroblast and immune-cell context.
- [Hu et al., population-based risk-gene study (2021)](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2005936)
  motivates distinguishing inherited variant evidence from tumor expression.
- [HGNC identity and nomenclature](https://www.genenames.org/)
  supplies approved human symbols; its data are [CC0](https://www.genenames.org/about/license/).
- [NCI biomarker explanation](https://www.cancer.gov/types/breast/diagnosis/breast-cancer-biomarker-tests)
  supplies the measurement distinction between RNA research and pathology tests.

No private selection table, ranks, paired cohort or unpublished effect estimate
is an authoring input. The catalog is fixed after the domain review and listed
alphabetically in the index. Inclusion does not imply that a gene is necessary,
sufficient, specific, clinically actionable or recently discovered.
