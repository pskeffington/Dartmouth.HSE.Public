## B. What this gene normally does

TOP2A helps untangle and manage DNA during replication and chromosome separation. Its activity includes controlled DNA cutting and rejoining. This molecular-function explanation is independently written and checked against the linked NCBI Gene record; it is separate from the cancer-study interpretation.

## C. Why breast cancer researchers study it

Learn how an enzyme can be a proliferation-associated signal and a pharmacological target without RNA being a response test.

STUB1-related experiments examined TOP2A ubiquitination and FOXM1-dependent transcription. This finding is confined to the study design and model described in section H.

## E. What the measurement means

Copy number, protein abundance, enzyme activity and drug effects are distinct observations.

DNA mutation changes sequence; copy number changes genomic dosage; RNA measures transcripts; total protein measures abundance; phosphorylation or another activity assay addresses regulation. A clinical laboratory result also depends on specimen, assay and validated criteria. None of these is a substitute for the others. See the [measurement guide](../GENE_EXPRESSION_INTERPRETATION.md).

## G. What this gene cannot tell us

TOP2A RNA alone cannot establish drug sensitivity or identify DNA amplification. Protein degradation, RNA and treatment response are distinct; retrospective tissue associations are not a standalone clinical test.
