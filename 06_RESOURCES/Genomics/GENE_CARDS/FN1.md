## B. What this gene normally does

FN1 encodes fibronectin, an extracellular protein that helps cells attach to and organize their surroundings. Its forms and assembly influence the matrix environment. This molecular-function explanation is independently written and checked against the linked NCBI Gene record; it is separate from the cancer-study interpretation.

## C. Why breast cancer researchers study it

Interpret tissue-remodeling signals that can come from stromal as well as epithelial cells.

Plasma-vesicle protein analyses included fibronectin alongside cellular follow-up. This finding is confined to the study design and model described in section H.

## D. Molecular subtype context

## E. What the measurement means

Protein deposition and matrix organization complement RNA measurements.

DNA mutation changes sequence; copy number changes genomic dosage; RNA measures transcripts; total protein measures abundance; phosphorylation or another activity assay addresses regulation. A clinical laboratory result also depends on specimen, assay and validated criteria. None of these is a substitute for the others. See the [measurement guide](../GENE_EXPRESSION_INTERPRETATION.md).
