## B. What this gene normally does

PIK3CA encodes a catalytic component of PI3K signaling. The enzyme changes membrane lipids that help recruit downstream signaling proteins. This molecular-function explanation is independently written and checked against the linked NCBI Gene record; it is separate from the cancer-study interpretation.

## C. Why breast cancer researchers study it

Follow a growth-signaling pathway across DNA variants, protein activity and clinical research.

PIK3CA was among recurrently mutated genes in breast tumors. This finding is confined to the study design and model described in section H.

## D. Molecular subtype context

TCGA reports enrichment of particular mutations in luminal A disease. This is a DNA finding, not a claim that high RNA separates luminal A from luminal B, HER2-enriched or basal-like disease. PAM50 uses a defined multi-gene expression method; clinical ER, PR and HER2 categories use separate assays. [Supervised risk predictor of breast cancer based on intrinsic subtypes. (2009)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19204204/).

## E. What the measurement means

A defined mutation assay is distinct from expression, copy number and downstream phosphorylation.

DNA mutation changes sequence; copy number changes genomic dosage; RNA measures transcripts; total protein measures abundance; phosphorylation or another activity assay addresses regulation. A clinical laboratory result also depends on specimen, assay and validated criteria. None of these is a substitute for the others. See the [measurement guide](../GENE_EXPRESSION_INTERPRETATION.md).
