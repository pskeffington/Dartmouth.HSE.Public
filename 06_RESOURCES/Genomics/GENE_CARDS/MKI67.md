## B. What this gene normally does

Ki-67 is associated with cycling cells and helps organize chromosomes during division. Its cellular distribution changes with cell-cycle stage. This molecular-function explanation is independently written and checked against the linked NCBI Gene record; it is separate from the cancer-study interpretation.

## C. Why breast cancer researchers study it

Separate a proliferation theme from a pathology labeling percentage or direct growth rate.

MKI67 RNA was examined for prognostic associations with an exploratory cutoff. This finding is confined to the study design and model described in section H.

## D. Molecular subtype context

The reviewed evidence does not establish a gene-specific discriminator across luminal A, luminal B, HER2-enriched and basal-like disease. The study context below should not be promoted to a four-subtype claim. PAM50 uses a defined multi-gene expression method; clinical ER, PR and HER2 categories use separate assays. [Supervised risk predictor of breast cancer based on intrinsic subtypes. (2009)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19204204/).

## E. What the measurement means

An IHC percentage counts stained nuclei under a defined protocol; RNA is a different quantity.

DNA mutation changes sequence; copy number changes genomic dosage; RNA measures transcripts; total protein measures abundance; phosphorylation or another activity assay addresses regulation. A clinical laboratory result also depends on specimen, assay and validated criteria. None of these is a substitute for the others. See the [measurement guide](../GENE_EXPRESSION_INTERPRETATION.md).

## F. Clinical and research relevance

Clinical laboratories measure ER, PR, HER2 and sometimes Ki-67 using defined tissue assays. This gene encodes a relevant product, but the RNA value is not the corresponding pathology result or its clinical threshold. For the established assay distinctions, see [NCI biomarker testing](https://www.cancer.gov/types/breast/diagnosis/breast-cancer-biomarker-tests).

## G. What this gene cannot tell us

MKI67 RNA is not a Ki-67 labeling index or a complete prognosis model. RNA thresholds are not Ki-67 immunohistochemistry percentages, and associations varied between cohorts.

## I. Student learning takeaway

1. **Function:** Ki-67 is associated with cycling cells and helps organize chromosomes during division.
2. **Measurement:** An IHC percentage counts stained nuclei under a defined protocol; RNA is a different quantity.
3. **Inference:** MKI67 RNA is not a Ki-67 labeling index or a complete prognosis model.

**Scientific question:** How would tumor-cell counting change an apparent bulk proliferation signal?

**Common mistake:** Treating the study's association or experimental endpoint as a validated individual diagnosis, subtype or treatment prediction.
