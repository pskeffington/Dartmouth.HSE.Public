## B. What this gene normally does

BCL2 can restrain mitochondrial cell-death signaling. Normal cells use such controls to avoid unnecessary loss while balancing survival against damage. This molecular-function explanation is independently written and checked against the linked NCBI Gene record; it is separate from the cancer-study interpretation.

## C. Why breast cancer researchers study it

Study the balance between survival signals and cell death rather than treating every survival protein as the same type of cancer marker.

Thiazolyl hydrazone experiments investigated cell death alongside modeled protein interactions. This finding is confined to the study design and model described in section H.

## E. What the measurement means

Compare BCL2 protein, interacting partners and a cell-death endpoint.

DNA mutation changes sequence; copy number changes genomic dosage; RNA measures transcripts; total protein measures abundance; phosphorylation or another activity assay addresses regulation. A clinical laboratory result also depends on specimen, assay and validated criteria. None of these is a substitute for the others. See the [measurement guide](../GENE_EXPRESSION_INTERPRETATION.md).
