## B. What this gene normally does

BAX participates in the mitochondrial route to programmed cell death. Activation and movement of the protein help control whether a stressed cell proceeds toward death. This molecular-function explanation is independently written and checked against the linked NCBI Gene record; it is separate from the cancer-study interpretation.

## C. Why breast cancer researchers study it

Investigate why a cell can contain BAX RNA yet remain alive after a stressor.

VALD-3 experiments implicated ROS/JNK/Bax signaling in GSDME-dependent cell death. This finding is confined to the study design and model described in section H.

## E. What the measurement means

Localization, protein conformation and downstream cell-death assays are more informative about activation than RNA alone.

DNA mutation changes sequence; copy number changes genomic dosage; RNA measures transcripts; total protein measures abundance; phosphorylation or another activity assay addresses regulation. A clinical laboratory result also depends on specimen, assay and validated criteria. None of these is a substitute for the others. See the [measurement guide](../GENE_EXPRESSION_INTERPRETATION.md).
