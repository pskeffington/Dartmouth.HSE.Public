## B. What this gene normally does

Cyclin E1 helps regulate the transition toward DNA replication with kinase partners. Normal control depends on when the protein is made and removed. This molecular-function explanation is independently written and checked against the linked NCBI Gene record; it is separate from the cancer-study interpretation.

## E. What the measurement means

Measure copy number and protein alongside RNA and cell-cycle fraction.

DNA mutation changes sequence; copy number changes genomic dosage; RNA measures transcripts; total protein measures abundance; phosphorylation or another activity assay addresses regulation. A clinical laboratory result also depends on specimen, assay and validated criteria. None of these is a substitute for the others. See the [measurement guide](../GENE_EXPRESSION_INTERPRETATION.md).

RNA overexpression alone cannot show that a particular kinase complex drives a tumor. Cohort RNA correlations and cell perturbations answer different questions; neither alone supplies a clinical cutoff.
