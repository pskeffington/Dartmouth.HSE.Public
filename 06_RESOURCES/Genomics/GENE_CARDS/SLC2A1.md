## B. What this gene normally does

SLC2A1 encodes GLUT1, which helps glucose cross cell membranes. Transport depends on protein placement and concentration gradients as well as transcript production. This molecular-function explanation is independently written and checked against the linked NCBI Gene record; it is separate from the cancer-study interpretation.

## C. Why breast cancer researchers study it

Distinguish a metabolic expression program from actual nutrient uptake.

EFO2/USF1 experiments investigated SLC2A1-related glycolysis and altered immune-cell killing. This finding is confined to the study design and model described in section H.

## E. What the measurement means

Surface transporter abundance, glucose uptake and metabolic flux are separate measurements.

DNA mutation changes sequence; copy number changes genomic dosage; RNA measures transcripts; total protein measures abundance; phosphorylation or another activity assay addresses regulation. A clinical laboratory result also depends on specimen, assay and validated criteria. None of these is a substitute for the others. See the [measurement guide](../GENE_EXPRESSION_INTERPRETATION.md).

## G. What this gene cannot tell us

SLC2A1 RNA cannot measure glycolytic flux or tissue oxygen availability. Transcript abundance cannot replace measured glucose flux; co-culture findings are not clinical treatment evidence.
