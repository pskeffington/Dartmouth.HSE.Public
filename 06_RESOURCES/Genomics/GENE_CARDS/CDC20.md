## B. What this gene normally does

CDC20 helps activate a complex that marks selected proteins for destruction during cell division. Controlled destruction permits chromosomes to progress through mitosis. This molecular-function explanation is independently written and checked against the linked NCBI Gene record; it is separate from the cancer-study interpretation.

## C. Why breast cancer researchers study it

Learn that cell-cycle control includes removing proteins, not just producing them.

Lnc-TRDMT1-5 experiments implicated an MSRB3/CDC20-related cell-cycle pathway. This finding is confined to the study design and model described in section H.

## D. Molecular subtype context

## E. What the measurement means

A mitotic fraction and degradation-pathway assay complement expression measurements.

DNA mutation changes sequence; copy number changes genomic dosage; RNA measures transcripts; total protein measures abundance; phosphorylation or another activity assay addresses regulation. A clinical laboratory result also depends on specimen, assay and validated criteria. None of these is a substitute for the others. See the [measurement guide](../GENE_EXPRESSION_INTERPRETATION.md).

## G. What this gene cannot tell us

CDC20 RNA does not establish that the chromosome-separation checkpoint is functioning correctly. A model-specific regulatory result does not prove that elevated CDC20 RNA causes all breast cancers.
