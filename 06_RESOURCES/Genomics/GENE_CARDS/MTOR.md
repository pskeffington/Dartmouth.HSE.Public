## B. What this gene normally does

MTOR participates in distinct protein complexes that coordinate growth, metabolism and stress responses. Activity depends on complex membership and upstream signals. This molecular-function explanation is independently written and checked against the linked NCBI Gene record; it is separate from the cancer-study interpretation.

## C. Why breast cancer researchers study it

Connect nutrient sensing to clinical research without using expression as a treatment-selection shortcut.

Everolimus plus exemestane improved progression-free survival in the studied population. This finding is confined to the study design and model described in section H.

## D. Molecular subtype context

The reviewed evidence does not establish a gene-specific discriminator across luminal A, luminal B, HER2-enriched and basal-like disease. The study context below should not be promoted to a four-subtype claim. PAM50 uses a defined multi-gene expression method; clinical ER, PR and HER2 categories use separate assays. [Supervised risk predictor of breast cancer based on intrinsic subtypes. (2009)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19204204/).

## E. What the measurement means

Downstream phosphorylation and complex-specific responses provide evidence beyond MTOR RNA.

DNA mutation changes sequence; copy number changes genomic dosage; RNA measures transcripts; total protein measures abundance; phosphorylation or another activity assay addresses regulation. A clinical laboratory result also depends on specimen, assay and validated criteria. None of these is a substitute for the others. See the [measurement guide](../GENE_EXPRESSION_INTERPRETATION.md).

## F. Clinical and research relevance

A randomized pathway-drug trial supplies clinical research evidence in a defined population. It does not by itself establish a current regulatory indication, isolate one gene as the drug mechanism or make transcript abundance a selection test. For the established assay distinctions, see [NCI biomarker testing](https://www.cancer.gov/types/breast/diagnosis/breast-cancer-biomarker-tests).

## G. What this gene cannot tell us

One transcript total cannot distinguish mTOR complex activities or establish drug response. A pathway-drug trial does not validate MTOR RNA as an eligibility test or measure kinase activation.
