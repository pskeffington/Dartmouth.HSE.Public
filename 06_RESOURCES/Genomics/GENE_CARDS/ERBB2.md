## B. What this gene normally does

ERBB2 encodes HER2, a receptor that helps transmit growth signals through receptor partnerships. Receptor quantity, gene copy number and phosphorylation are different layers of biology. This molecular-function explanation is independently written and checked against the linked NCBI Gene record; it is separate from the cancer-study interpretation.

## C. Why breast cancer researchers study it

Understand why clinical HER2 assessment and an RNA-defined HER2-enriched label are related but distinct.

HER2-enriched tumors showed a characteristic molecular context including HER-family signaling. This finding is confined to the study design and model described in section H.

## D. Molecular subtype context

TCGA reports HER-family signaling within HER2-enriched tumors. This does not make ERBB2 RNA a classifier separating luminal A, luminal B or basal-like tumors, nor make HER2-enriched identical to clinical HER2-positive. PAM50 uses a defined multi-gene expression method; clinical ER, PR and HER2 categories use separate assays. [Supervised risk predictor of breast cancer based on intrinsic subtypes. (2009)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19204204/).

## E. What the measurement means

Clinical HER2 protein and amplification testing follow defined assay and scoring standards.

DNA mutation changes sequence; copy number changes genomic dosage; RNA measures transcripts; total protein measures abundance; phosphorylation or another activity assay addresses regulation. A clinical laboratory result also depends on specimen, assay and validated criteria. None of these is a substitute for the others. See the [measurement guide](../GENE_EXPRESSION_INTERPRETATION.md).

## F. Clinical and research relevance

Clinical laboratories measure ER, PR, HER2 and sometimes Ki-67 using defined tissue assays. This gene encodes a relevant product, but the RNA value is not the corresponding pathology result or its clinical threshold. For the established assay distinctions, see [NCI biomarker testing](https://www.cancer.gov/types/breast/diagnosis/breast-cancer-biomarker-tests).

## G. What this gene cannot tell us

ERBB2 RNA alone cannot establish HER2 status, subtype or treatment eligibility. Expression-defined HER2-enriched status does not equal pathology-defined HER2 positivity; clinical assays require their own criteria.
