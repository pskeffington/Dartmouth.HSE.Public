## B. What this gene normally does

ATM helps cells recognize DNA damage and coordinate repair or a pause in division. It is a signaling controller rather than a repair enzyme that directly replaces damaged bases. This molecular-function explanation is independently written and checked against the linked NCBI Gene record; it is separate from the cancer-study interpretation.

## C. Why breast cancer researchers study it

Learn why inherited variant studies and tumor stress responses answer different questions.

Pathogenic germline ATM variants were associated with breast cancer risk, particularly ER-positive disease. This finding is confined to the study design and model described in section H.

## D. Molecular subtype context

The reviewed evidence does not establish a gene-specific discriminator across luminal A, luminal B, HER2-enriched and basal-like disease. The study context below should not be promoted to a four-subtype claim. PAM50 uses a defined multi-gene expression method; clinical ER, PR and HER2 categories use separate assays. [Supervised risk predictor of breast cancer based on intrinsic subtypes. (2009)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19204204/).

## E. What the measurement means

A DNA variant assay asks about sequence; phosphorylation assays ask about a damage response.

DNA mutation changes sequence; copy number changes genomic dosage; RNA measures transcripts; total protein measures abundance; phosphorylation or another activity assay addresses regulation. A clinical laboratory result also depends on specimen, assay and validated criteria. None of these is a substitute for the others. See the [measurement guide](../GENE_EXPRESSION_INTERPRETATION.md).

## F. Clinical and research relevance

Inherited-variant research concerns classified DNA variants, with risk depending on gene, variant and population. Genetic counseling and validated testing are separate from this educational RNA reference. For the established assay distinctions, see [NCI biomarker testing](https://www.cancer.gov/types/breast/diagnosis/breast-cancer-biomarker-tests).

## G. What this gene cannot tell us

An ATM variant of uncertain significance is not equivalent to a pathogenic inherited variant. Risk pertains to classified DNA variants and population ascertainment, not high or low ATM expression.

## H. Literature and evidence

**Primary study:** [A Population-Based Study of Genes Previously Implicated in Breast Cancer. (2021)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33471974/). DOI: [10.1056/nejmoa2005936](https://doi.org/10.1056/nejmoa2005936).

**Design:** Population-based case-control sequencing. **Population/model:** 32,247 breast cancer cases and 32,544 controls.

**Narrow supported claim:** Pathogenic germline ATM variants were associated with breast cancer risk, particularly ER-positive disease.

**Important limit:** Risk pertains to classified DNA variants and population ascertainment, not high or low ATM expression.
