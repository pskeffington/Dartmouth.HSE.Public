## B. What this gene normally does

RB1 restrains transcriptional programs that help cells enter DNA replication. Phosphorylation and protein integrity affect whether that restraint remains in place. This molecular-function explanation is independently written and checked against the linked NCBI Gene record; it is separate from the cancer-study interpretation.

## C. Why breast cancer researchers study it

Explore how a downstream gate can alter responses to upstream cell-cycle inhibition.

Phosphate experiments examined RB1-E2F-related transcription and repair responses. This finding is confined to the study design and model described in section H.

## D. Molecular subtype context

The reviewed evidence does not establish a gene-specific discriminator across luminal A, luminal B, HER2-enriched and basal-like disease. The study context below should not be promoted to a four-subtype claim. PAM50 uses a defined multi-gene expression method; clinical ER, PR and HER2 categories use separate assays. [Supervised risk predictor of breast cancer based on intrinsic subtypes. (2009)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19204204/).

## E. What the measurement means

DNA sequence, total RB protein and phosphorylation are different measurements.

DNA mutation changes sequence; copy number changes genomic dosage; RNA measures transcripts; total protein measures abundance; phosphorylation or another activity assay addresses regulation. A clinical laboratory result also depends on specimen, assay and validated criteria. None of these is a substitute for the others. See the [measurement guide](../GENE_EXPRESSION_INTERPRETATION.md).

## G. What this gene cannot tell us

RB1 RNA cannot establish a functional checkpoint or predict inhibitor response. Expression changes under exposure and serum associations do not demonstrate intact RB1 protein function.

## I. Student learning takeaway

1. **Function:** RB1 restrains transcriptional programs that help cells enter DNA replication.
2. **Measurement:** DNA sequence, total RB protein and phosphorylation are different measurements.
3. **Inference:** RB1 RNA cannot establish a functional checkpoint or predict inhibitor response.

**Scientific question:** What happens when an upstream kinase is inhibited but the downstream gate is missing?

**Common mistake:** Treating the study's association or experimental endpoint as a validated individual diagnosis, subtype or treatment prediction.
